n.002 — about — n.002

I'm Pearlyn Elizabeth — an AI systems architect with an MSc in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Surrey. I build the layer most demos skip: production-grade RAG, multi-agent orchestration, and secure LLM gateways that survive contact with real users and real data.

My work covers hybrid retrieval (dense + sparse + reranking), agent frameworks, and cloud infrastructure — provisioning on Google Cloud, wiring Vertex AI and Gemini model endpoints, and hardening the pipeline with PII detection and prompt-injection defence.

I also bring a commercial lens. I can scope what to build, ship it, and explain why it matters to the business — which is why my systems get adopted, not archived.
